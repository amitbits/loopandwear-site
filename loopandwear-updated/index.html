Our Story

From India to Your Doorstep

My name is Ayaan (I'm a junior in high school), and this is Nani's story as much as it's mine. When she came to live with us from India, the transition was hard. New country, new language, far from friends.

Knitting was always her therapy. I thought - what if I could help her turn something she loves into a way to meet people here?

"When I knit, I feel at home anywhere in the world. Each piece is a little piece of my heart."

So Loop & Wear started — a small project where Nani's hands and my hustle team up to make something real for people around us.

The Artist

Nani — The Heart of Loop & Wear

Nani has been knitting for over 40 years, learning the craft in India where it was just part of daily life. When she moved to the US, knitting was one of the only things that still felt familiar.

Through Loop & Wear, Nani has found her community here. She loves knowing that each piece she creates brings warmth to a new family, and that her lifelong skill is now connecting her with neighbors she'd never have met otherwise.

💛 "I feel connected, useful, and happy. Every order makes my day."
Teen Entrepreneur

Ayaan — The Business Mind

I'm a high school junior who started Loop & Wear with one goal: help Nani feel at home in a new country by turning her passion into purpose. I handle everything from the website to customer orders to marketing.

Running this business has taught me more than any class could — about entrepreneurship, community, and what it means to give back. Seeing Nani light up when an order comes in makes every late night worth it.

FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

How long does a custom order take?

Typically 2–4 weeks depending on complexity and Nani's current queue. Simple items like socks or mufflars can be done in 1–2 weeks. We'll give you an estimate when you place your order.

What yarn do you use?

Nani uses high-quality wool and acrylic blends — warm, durable, and soft. For kids or those with sensitivities, we have hypoallergenic yarn options. Just let us know!

Do you ship outside California?

Yes! We ship anywhere in the US. Local Bay Area customers can also arrange pickup.

How do I pay?

Since every piece is personalized, call or text us at 408-726-1512 to arrange payment. We accept Venmo, Zelle, or PayPal.

Can I return or exchange?

Because each piece is handmade to order, we can't offer refunds — but if something isn't right, we'll fix it. Customer satisfaction means everything to us.

Can I give this as a gift?

Absolutely — it's one of the most heartfelt gifts you can give. We include a handwritten note from Nani with every order and can gift-wrap upon request.
